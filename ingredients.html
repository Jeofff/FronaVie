Filipino Botanicals

Sampaguita

Used in FronaVie as a gentle ferment. Traditionally worn as a garland flower across the Philippines, chosen here for its light, calming scent profile and its role in our barrier serum.

Featured in Revive

Calamansi

A small citrus common in Filipino kitchens, long used for brightening. FronaVie uses a stabilized extract for tone and dullness, in place of the raw juice most households already reach for.
