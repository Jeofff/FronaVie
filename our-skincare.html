Ingredient Storytelling

Every ingredient has a reason.

Sampaguita

Ferment · Philippines

Used as a gentle ferment, chosen for its light, calming scent and its role in our barrier serum. Traditionally worn as a garland flower across the Philippines.

Calamansi

Stabilized Extract · Philippines

A small citrus common in Filipino kitchens, long used for brightening. We use a stabilized extract for tone and dullness, in place of the raw juice most households already reach for.
