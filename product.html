Why Your Skin May Love It

Comfort first, glow after.

Most serums ask dry skin to feel silky right away and settle for a flat finish instead. This one leans the other way. It sinks in slowly, sits comfortably under a moisturizer, and lets the radiance build over days rather than promising it overnight.

Texture

Silk-gel. Not watery, not heavy.

A gel that turns silky on contact and sets down without pilling. Wears well under sunscreen and does not fight with makeup applied after it.

How To Use

Three steps, morning and night.

  • 01Apply after cleansing, on skin that is still a little damp.
  • 02Warm two to three drops between your palms, then press gently into the face and neck.
  • 03Follow with a moisturizer if your skin wants a heavier finish, especially at night.

Ingredient Story

The flower our grandmothers strung into garlands.

Sampaguita has carried meaning in the Philippines for generations, offered at altars, sold at intersections, worn to weddings. This serum is our attempt to put it to work rather than print it on a label. Read more on the ingredients page.

Questions

A few things people usually ask.

Can I use this if my skin is sensitive?

It was formulated to be gentle, but everyone's skin is different. We recommend a small patch test first, especially if you know your skin reacts easily.

Can I use it both morning and night?

Yes. Most people use it twice daily, though some prefer it only at night if their skin runs oily during the day.

What should I use it with?

It pairs naturally with the Sampaguita Milk Cleanser before it, and the Calamansi Night Cream after it in the evening.

How long before I notice a difference?

Comfort is usually the first thing people notice, within a few days. Visible radiance tends to build over two to four weeks of consistent use. We do not have formal clinical data to cite yet.
