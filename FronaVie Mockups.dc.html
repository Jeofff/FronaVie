OUR STORY

Care that honors
where it came from

It started with the women who never went first.

FronaVie began in a kitchen in Manila, with a question that had no good answer: why did every brightening product on the shelf ask you to damage your skin to be seen?

The women in our family had been mixing rice water, calamansi and coconut oil for generations, rituals that were gentle by instinct. What they never had was the science to make those rituals reliable. That is the gap we set out to close.

So we work the way a good formulator and a good grandmother would agree on: ingredients chosen for what they do, in amounts that respect the barrier, proven in genuine heat and humidity, on the Filipino skin it was made for.

The actives

Ingredient
macro

Kojic Acid

2% · FROM RICE FERMENTATION

A by-product of fermented rice, the same tradition behind heirloom rice water rinses.

WHAT IT DOES

Slows tyrosinase, the enzyme that triggers excess melanin. Existing marks fade as skin renews; your natural tone is untouched.

WHAT IT WILL NOT DO

It will not bleach skin, and it cannot outpace sun exposure, so sunscreen is never optional.

Ingredient
macro

Niacinamide

4% · VITAMIN B3

The best-studied barrier ingredient in modern skincare.

WHAT IT DOES

Supports ceramide production and calms redness, so a brightening routine can be used daily without irritation building up.

WHAT IT WILL NOT DO

It is not an exfoliant, , and 4% is deliberate: higher is not gentler.

Ingredient
macro

Sodium Hyaluronate

1% · LOW MOLECULAR WEIGHT

The salt form of hyaluronic acid: smaller, and far better suited to a rinse off.

WHAT IT DOES

Holds water in the upper layers of skin so the face feels cushioned, not tight, after rinsing.

WHAT IT WILL NOT DO

It does not replace a moisturizer in humid weather, or in air conditioning.
