Where It Comes From

The flowers we know. The ingredients we grew up with. Reimagined for modern skin.

Sampaguita has always meant something in the Philippines. Offered at altars, sold at intersections, worn to weddings. We didn't want to print it on a label. We wanted to actually put it to work.

Calamansi does the brightening most Filipino kitchens already understood, long before "vitamin C" became a shelf label.

Our Story
"I started FronaVie because I got tired of choosing between what my skin needed and what felt like mine."
Maribeth Roa, Founder of FronaVie

Maribeth built FronaVie around a simple frustration. The skincare she could find either ignored where she came from, or leaned on it as decoration. She wanted formulas that took Filipino botanicals as seriously as any imported active, and a brand her own mother would recognize as respectful, not borrowed.
