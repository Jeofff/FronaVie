Where It Comes From

The flowers we know. The ingredients we grew up with. Reimagined for modern skin.

Sampaguita has always meant something in the Philippines. Offered at altars, sold at intersections, worn to weddings. We didn't want to print it on a label. We wanted to actually put it to work.

Calamansi does the brightening most Filipino kitchens already understood, long before "vitamin C" became a shelf label.
