Our Story

Why FronaVie exists, in her own words.

Why did caring for her skin always feel like picking a side, between what worked and what felt like hers?

Plate: Maribeth Roa. Photography in progress.
"I started FronaVie because I got tired of choosing between what my skin needed and what felt like mine."
Maribeth Roa, Founder of FronaVie

Maribeth grew up around the small, practical rituals most Filipino households already knew: calamansi cut into halves for uneven spots, sampaguita worn on special mornings, coconut oil passed down as the answer to almost everything. None of it was ever branded. It was just how things were done.

Years later, working in skincare retail, she kept noticing the same gap. The imported brands she sold were effective but felt distant, built for someone else's skin and someone else's climate. The local products that leaned on Filipino identity often stopped at the packaging. Few treated the ingredients themselves as worth the same rigor as an imported active.

FronaVie is her answer to that gap: a line built around Filipino botanicals, formulated with the same seriousness as any international brand, and named after no one but the brand itself.

Every formula carries a little of where Maribeth comes from. The rest is simply careful, modern work.

Maribeth Roa, Founder
The Belief

Effective doesn't have to mean imported. Personal doesn't have to mean homemade.

FronaVie sits between two worlds Maribeth refused to keep separate: the clinical rigor of modern formulation, and the quiet, practical wisdom of Filipino skincare traditions. Neither one wins. Both are the point.
